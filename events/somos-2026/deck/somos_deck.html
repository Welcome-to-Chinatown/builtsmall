Section one

What is AI

The foundation. What it is, how it works, and the words worth knowing before you use it.

The core concept

What is an LLM

Large Language Model
A program trained on a massive amount of human language, trillions of words, most of the public internet and more. That training is why it reads and writes the way people do, and predicts the next word so well.
The name is the definition: Large is the huge training data, Language is human writing, Model predicts the next word. Every chat assistant you know, ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, is one.
Good to know

The vocabulary of AI

Prompt
Your instruction to the AI. Prompt engineering is shaping that instruction to get a good answer.
Context window
The AI's working memory for one conversation. Your prompt, any files, and its reply all share the same space.
Tokens
The pieces of text the AI counts, roughly a word each. More tokens, more it costs.
RAG
Give the AI your own documents to answer from. Claude Projects and custom GPTs do this, no coding.
Agentic AI
AI that takes actions, browses, fills forms, not just answers.
Open source
Models like Llama, DeepSeek, or Qwen that you can run on your own device. Private, but technical to set up.
Ground the room

What AI is, and is not

It is
  • Predictive. It guesses the most likely next word, based on patterns it has seen.
  • A fast drafter. Quick, tireless, confident. You decide what ships.
  • A tool that reads and writes. Newer tools also create documents, images, even spreadsheets.
It is not
  • Magic. It does not understand. It pattern-matches, and it can be confidently wrong.
  • Aware of your business unless you tell it.
  • A replacement for your judgment.

Section two

Find your PITA

Before you prompt, you find the task worth prompting. Start with the work that drains your week.

Warm up

Make toast

Do this first

Tell me how to make toast.

  • The task. Call out the exact steps, one at a time.
  • The catch. I am a robot that has never seen food. I do exactly what you say, word for word, nothing more.
The lesson: AI only knows what you spell out — the same reason clear steps make a prompt work.
Find the draining task

Start with a PITA

Start with a PITA.

Pain In The Ass. The task you do again and again that quietly eats your week.

Not the big one-time project. The small repeat one.

Sounds like
  • Re-typing the same intake form
  • Drafting the same client email
  • Sorting the same expenses every month
  • Writing another version of the same post
The rule

Do it once, skip it. Do it every week, audit it.

Repetition is the signal. If the task keeps coming back, it is worth a look.

Is it AI-shaped?

The three filters

1
Repetitive
You do it the same way, again and again.
Yes the weekly reportNo a one-time decision
2
Text or data
It is writing, sorting, or summarizing information.
Yes emails, lists, forms, numbersNo a handshake or a phone call
3
Describable
You can write the steps down for someone brand new.
Yes you can explain howNo it is pure gut feel
Three yes answers means you have found an AI-shaped problem.

Section three

Shape the ask

Four parts that turn a vague request into an answer you can actually use.

The framework

The four-part prompt

PartWhat to doExample
Role & GoalTell AI who it is and what to accomplishYou are my bookkeeper. Sort last month's expenses.
StepsBreak the task into clear numbered steps1. Group by category. 2. Total each. 3. Flag anything odd.
OutputSay exactly what you want backA table I can paste into a spreadsheet.
EvalAsk AI to check its own workConfirm every expense is counted once.
Start with a plain ask, then add the parts it is missing: who it is, the steps, the output you want, and a check. Same framework, sharper each time.
The reveal

Watch the ask change the answer

Low level prompt
"Write a caption for an ice cream shop."
Generic. It could belong to any shop, anywhere.
High level prompt
Role & goal: You are Soft Swerve's social manager. Write an Instagram caption for the new ube coconut swirl.
Steps: name the flavor, make it playful, end with a call to visit.
Output: under 30 words, in English and Chinese.
Eval: check it sounds like a small shop, not a big brand.
Specific. You could post it today.
The quality of your input sets the quality of your output.

Lead with the human

Safety and privacy

01
Keep a human in the loop. AI drafts, you decide. Never let it send money, sign, hire, or post on its own.
02
Keep private things out. No card or account numbers, passwords, SSNs, or customer and employee records in public AI tools.
03
Assume free tools may learn from what you type. Turn off training in settings — we'll show you exactly how next.
04
AI makes things up. Verify names, numbers, dates, and anything legal, tax, or medical with a person.
New scams use AI voice cloning and better phishing. Verify any unusual money or access request by phone.
Protect your data

Turn off training

Most chat assistants can stop training on what you type. Here is exactly where to find the setting.

ChatGPT
Settings › Data controls › turn off "Improve the model for everyone."
Claude
Settings › Privacy › turn off "Help improve our AI models."
Gemini
Settings › Gemini Apps Activity › turn it Off.
