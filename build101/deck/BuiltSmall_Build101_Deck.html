Good to know

The vocabulary of AI

Prompt
Your instruction to the AI. Prompt engineering is shaping that instruction to get a good answer.
Context window
The AI's working memory for one conversation. Your prompt, any files, and its reply all share the same space.
Tokens
The pieces of text the AI counts, roughly a word each. More tokens, more it costs.
RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)
Give the AI your own documents to answer from. Example: upload your menu or policies to NotebookLM and ask it questions. Claude Projects and custom GPTs work the same way. No coding.
Agentic AI
AI that takes actions, browses, fills forms, not just answers.
Open source
Models like Llama, DeepSeek, Qwen, or Kimi K2 that you can run on your own device. Private, but technical to set up.

Part one

The workflow audit

Before you prompt, you find the task worth prompting.

Warm up · 5 minutes

Make a sandwich

Do this first

Make a sandwich.

  • The task. Write the exact steps to make a peanut butter sandwich.
  • The catch. I am a robot that has never seen food. I do exactly what you say, word for word.
Find the draining task

Start with a PITA

Start with a PITA.

Pain In The Ass. The task you do again and again that quietly eats your week.

Not the big one-time project. The small repeat one.

Sounds like
  • Re-typing the same intake form
  • Drafting the same client email
  • Sorting the same expenses every month
  • Writing another version of the same post
The rule

Do it once, skip it. Do it every week, audit it.

Repetition is the signal. If the task keeps coming back, it is worth a look.

A real example · Soft Swerve

From vague to buildable

The vague ask
"I want a tool that shows me financial and sales data across my six stores."
True, but too broad to build. A wish, not a task.
The buildable problem
"I spend 6 hours a month reconciling tip wages for part-time staff across 6 stores. If I had a dashboard that did it, I would get that time back and see sales in real time."
Specific task. Real number. Real outcome.
PITA is the questions that get you from the wish to the task. We asked Jason how he does it now, step by step, and where it breaks.

From a vague wish to a dashboard he checks every day.
Once the PITA was clear, this is what we built: sales by day and hour across all six stores.
  • He sees when each store is busy at a glance.
  • The 6 hours a month of reconciling are gone.
Is it AI-shaped?

The three filters

1
Repetitive
You do it the same way, again and again.
Yes the weekly reportNo a one-time decision
2
Text or data
It is writing, sorting, or summarizing information.
Yes emails, lists, forms, numbersNo a handshake or a phone call
3
Describable
You can write the steps down for someone brand new.
Yes you can explain howNo it is pure gut feel
Three yes answers means you have found an AI-shaped problem.
How to run it

The audit, step by step

1
Pick a task. Something you do every week, not once.
2
Describe it step by step. Same move as the sandwich: write it like instructions for a robot that has never done it.
3
Time it. Minutes per run, times per week. The hours add up fast.
4
Name the PITA. The slowest, most repetitive, most copy-paste step. You will test it against the filters next.
Name it in one line

The problem statement

My pain point is that [the task].
If I had [a better way],
I would [the impact].
Name your PITA in one line. This is the sentence you build on.
Your turn · 10 minutes

Find your PITA, in pairs

  • 2 min, on your own. Fill in the first steps on the PITA Canvas.
  • 3 min, pair up. One is the owner, one is the detective: ask questions, do not solve.
  • 2 min, switch. Now the other partner is the owner.
  • 1 min, write it. My pain point is that ___. If I had ___, I would ___.
  • 2 min, out loud. A few of you say your PITA to the room.
Keep this sentence. You will use it when you write your own prompt.

Part two

Shaping the ask

Three moves that turn a vague request into a useful answer.

Two words, defined

Prompt vs prompt engineering

Prompt
What you type into the AI. Your request, in plain words.
Prompt engineering
Shaping that request so the answer comes back useful. Same model, better ask, better result.
You already do this. Today we make it deliberate.
The framework

Prompt Framework

PartWhat to doExample
Role & GoalTell AI who it is and what to accomplishYou are my bookkeeper. Sort last month's expenses.
StepsBreak the task into clear numbered steps1. Group by category. 2. Total each. 3. Flag anything odd.
OutputSay exactly what you want backA table I can paste into a spreadsheet.
EvalAsk AI to check its own workConfirm every expense is counted once.
Start with a plain ask, then add the parts it is missing: who it is, the steps, the output you want, and a check. Same framework, sharper each time.
The reveal

Watch the ask change the answer

Low level prompt
"Write a caption for an ice cream shop."
Generic. It could belong to any shop, anywhere.
High level prompt
Role & goal: You are Soft Swerve's social manager. Write an Instagram caption for the new ube coconut swirl.
Steps: name the flavor, make it playful, end with a call to visit.
Output: under 30 words, in English and Chinese.
Eval: check it sounds like a small shop, not a big brand.
Specific. You could post it today.
The quality of your input sets the quality of your output.

Build the dashboard

Open the handout, download the sample file, attach it to a new chat, then fill in the four-part template. You write the Steps.
Fill in the blanksRole & goal: You are my business analyst. I run [type of shop] and want a dashboard of how it is doing. Steps: ______ (clean the dates, merge the category names, handle refunds, then the math) Output: Four number cards, a bar chart, a line chart, a table. Plain labels. Eval: Check the totals against the file and tell me the cleaning choices you made.
Check your numbers: revenue ~$3,825, 100 orders, avg ~$38, refunds $321.52, six categories. Then make one change — add a feature, change a color, add a filter.

Your turn · 10 minutes

Now write your own

Pick a task you want AI to do — your PITA, or anything else. The hack: let AI write the prompt with you.
  • In a new chat, paste the four-part framework and say: “Interview me about this task, then write the prompt for me.”
  • Answer its questions. It turns your answers into a finished four-part prompt.
  • Copy that prompt into a fresh chat and run it. Reply to fix anything off.
Use your real details. Never paste passwords or account numbers.

Open it anytime
Next week it already has your prompt and your file. No re-typing.
